READ & UNDERSTAND

Lesson 课文

Before you read

Listen then answer this question. Why do the children thank their mother?

听录音，然后回答问题。为什么孩子们向母亲致谢？

  1. 01

    Mother

    What’s the matter, children?

    怎么啦，孩子们？

  2. 02

    Girl

    We’re tired …

    我们累了……

  3. 03

    Boy

    … and thirsty, Mum.

    ……口也渴，妈妈。

  1. 04

    Mother

    Sit down here.

    坐在这儿吧。

  1. 05

    Mother

    Are you all right now?

    你们现在好些了吗？

  2. 06

    Boy

    No, we aren’t.

    不，还没有。

  1. 07

    Mother

    Look!

    瞧！

  2. 08

    Mother

    There’s an ice cream man.

    有个卖冰淇淋的。

  1. 09

    Mother

    Two ice creams please.

    请拿两份冰淇淋。

  1. 10

    Mother

    Here you are, children.

    拿着，孩子们。

  2. 11

    Children

    Thanks, Mum.

    谢谢，妈妈。

  1. 12

    Girl

    These ice creams are nice.

    这些冰淇淋真好吃。

  1. 13

    Mother

    Are you all right now?

    你们现在好了吧？

  2. 14

    Children

    Yes, we are, thank you!

    是的，现在好了，谢谢您！

Notes on the text 课文注释

01 · What’s the matter?

= Tell me what’s wrong. 怎么啦？

02 · There’s = There is

There’s 是 There is 的缩写形式。
