Notes on the text 课文注释

01 · What colour’s = What colour is

What colour’s 是 What colour is 的缩写。

02 · Come upstairs and see it.

句中 and 不当“和”讲，而是表示目的。例如：Come and see me. 来见我。原书在此要求使用 Come upstairs and see it.，而不是 Come upstairs to see it.。
