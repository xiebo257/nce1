A fine day

晴天

Describe the weather and what a family is doing by the river.
描述天气以及一家人在河边的活动。

01

READ & UNDERSTAND

Lesson 课文

Before you read

Listen then answer this question. Where is the Jones family?

听录音，然后回答问题。琼斯一家人在哪里？

  1. 01

    It is a fine day today.

    今天天气好。

  2. 02

    There are some clouds in the sky, but the sun is shining.

    天空中飘着几朵云，但阳光灿烂。

  3. 03

    Mr. Jones is with his family.

    琼斯先生同他的家人在一起。

  4. 04

    They are walking over the bridge.

    他们正在过桥。

  5. 05

    There are some boats on the river.

    河上有几艘船。

  6. 06

    Mr. Jones and his wife are looking at them.

    琼斯先生和他的妻子正在看这些船。

  7. 07

    Sally is looking at a big ship.

    萨莉正在看一艘大船。

  8. 08

    The ship is going under the bridge.

    那船正从桥下驶过。

  9. 09

    Tim is looking at an aeroplane.

    蒂姆正望着一架飞机。

  10. 10

    The aeroplane is flying over the river.

    飞机正从河上飞过。

Notes on the text 课文注释

01 · It is a fine day today.

句中的 it 指天气。

02 · some clouds

几朵云。some 可以修饰可数名词复数，也能修饰不可数名词。
