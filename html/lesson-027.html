Mrs. Smith’s living room

史密斯太太的客厅

Describe a living room and practise there is, there are and words for position.
介绍客厅，练习 there is、there are 和方位词。

01

READ & UNDERSTAND

Lesson 课文

Before you read

Listen then answer this question. Where are the books?

听录音，然后回答问题。书在哪里？

  1. 01

    Mrs. Smith’s living room is large.

    史密斯太太的客厅很大。

  2. 02

    There is a television in the room.

    客厅里有台电视机。

  3. 03

    The television is near the window.

    电视机靠近窗子。

  4. 04

    There are some magazines on the television.

    电视机上放着几本杂志。

  5. 05

    There is a table in the room.

    客厅里有张桌子。

  6. 06

    There are some newspapers on the table.

    桌上放着几份报纸。

  7. 07

    There are some armchairs in the room.

    客厅里有几把扶手椅。

  8. 08

    The armchairs are near the table.

    这些扶手椅靠近桌子。

  9. 09

    There is a stereo in the room.

    客厅里有台立体声音响。

  10. 10

    The stereo is near the door.

    音响靠近门。

  11. 11

    There are some books on the stereo.

    音响上面有几本书。

  12. 12

    There are some pictures in the room.

    客厅里有几幅画。

  13. 13

    The pictures are on the wall.

    画挂在墙上。

Notes on the text 课文注释

01 · There are …

There are 的结构中要用复数名词。

02 · near the window (door)

靠近窗（门），为介词短语，在本句中作表语。

03 · on the wall

在墙上。介词短语作表语。
